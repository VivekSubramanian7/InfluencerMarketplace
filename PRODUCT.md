# Product

## Register

brand

(Brand-first: the landing page and public creator storefronts are the product's face and must carry real identity. App surfaces — dashboard, deals, discovery, admin — inherit a quieter, workflow-serving cut of the same system.)

## Users

- **Video micro-influencers** (1k–100k followers on TikTok / YouTube / Instagram Reels): checking deals between shoots, often on a phone, energized but time-poor. Job: look professional to brands, get booked, get paid without chasing.
- **Brand/marketing managers** at small-to-mid companies: browsing on a laptop in work hours. Job: find credible video creators fast, book without DM chaos, trust that stats and delivery are real.
- **Platform admin** (operator): resolving disputes and reports; needs clarity over beauty.

## Product Purpose

A marketplace ("Clipline" — working name) where video creators publish bookable storefronts with productized offerings and brands discover, book, and track deals through a state machine with anti-ghosting timers, messaging, and two-sided reviews. Success = a creator's storefront makes them look more professional than a media kit, and a brand's booking feels safer than a DM.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, creator-native, precise.** Made by people who get the creator economy — bold type and committed color, but the deal machinery underneath is exact and trustworthy. Energy without chaos; the confidence of someone who knows their rate.

## Anti-references

- **Heepsy / generic influencer-SaaS**: take their UX patterns (filter chips, metric cards, verification checkmarks), never their green-CTA white-SaaS identity.
- **Generic purple-gradient startup** (Linktree-adjacent): no AI-purple, no gradient text.
- **Dark crypto-bro dashboards**: no neon-on-black, no glassmorphism.
- **Corporate enterprise gray**: no dense gray tables with default blue buttons.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is visible.** Verification states, escrow/off-platform banners, timers, and audit trails are design features, not fine print — the anti-scam research is the brand story.
2. **The creator is the hero.** Storefronts frame the creator's identity and rate with confidence; the platform's own chrome stays behind them.
3. **One system, two volumes.** Brand surfaces run the palette loud (committed crimson); workflow surfaces run the same tokens quiet. Nothing is off-system.
4. **State is always legible.** Every deal screen answers "where is this deal and whose move is it?" at a glance.
5. **No costume.** No fake stats, no decorative jargon, no mono-as-tech-cosplay; every visual claim maps to a real mechanism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, keyboard navigable, visible focus states, labeled inputs, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation. Tap targets ≥44px on mobile.
